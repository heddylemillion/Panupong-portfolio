01 / About

Technology should have a soul.

I am a Bangkok University student fascinated by the space where hardware mechanics, software, culture, and people meet.

My work started with IoT circuits designed in KiCad, 3D printing, and a curiosity for how machines move. It grew into robotics, computer vision, PLC programming, sound design, and music production. That mix gives me a multidisciplinary way to approach real-world problems: understand the human need, then build the system that makes a meaningful difference.

I believe the strongest innovations are not trapped in a laboratory. They should answer business needs, make technology more approachable, and create a sustainable impact for the people who use them.
